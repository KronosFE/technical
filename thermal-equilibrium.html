Thermal Equilibrium

When plasma species share a common temperature via collisions.

Glossary of Fusion & Plasma TermsUpdated 2026-08-11

Given enough collisions, a plasma's particles relax toward a common (Maxwellian) temperature. Whether ions and electrons are in equilibrium with each other affects heating strategy and radiation.
