How it works · live

Fuel follows purpose

Deuterium and tritium fuse in the core. Neutrons stream outward into the lithium blanket, breeding new tritium that returns to the fuel loop — a self-sustaining cycle.

Live · pink: D–T core · blue: 14 MeV neutrons · gold: bred tritium returning
The breeder · fuel

The breeding blanket

Each 14 MeV neutron, captured in the lithium blanket, breeds a fresh tritium atom.

Live · n + ⁶Li → T + ⁴He · advanced blanket TBR 1.42 → ~2.0 kg-T/yr per unit
The burner · power

The tandem mirror

High-field plugs trap the D–³He plasma; charged exhaust becomes electricity in a direct converter — no steam cycle.
